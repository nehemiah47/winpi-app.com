Privacy Policy - WinPi

Last updated: November 2026

WinPi operates on the Pi Network ecosystem and respects user privacy. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

1. Information We Use

When you open WinPi inside Pi Browser, Pi SDK and Pi Sign-In may be used to verify your session and support gameplay features.

2. Local Storage

Your browser may store basic interface settings, such as display currency preferences, to keep the app responsive.

3. Contact & Home

For questions about privacy, reach out through our Pi Developer Portal listing or return to WinPi home.
